KYC POLICY - Standard Operating Procedure v2.1
Updated: January 2025

1. CUSTOMER IDENTIFICATION
All new customers must provide valid government-issued photo ID.
Accepted documents: Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Passport, Voter ID.

2. ADDRESS VERIFICATION
Proof of address must be submitted within 30 days of account opening.
Accepted: Utility bill, Bank statement, Rental agreement (not older than 3 months).

3. EXCEPTION HANDLING
If a customer cannot provide standard KYC documents, escalate to the Branch Manager.
The Branch Manager may approve alternative documentation on a case-by-case basis.
Document the exception in the CRM with reason code EXC-KYC-01.

4. RENEWAL
KYC must be renewed every 5 years for regular customers.
High-risk customers require annual KYC renewal.
